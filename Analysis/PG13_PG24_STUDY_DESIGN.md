# PG-13 / PG-24 Controlled Auditory Study Design

## Objective

Determine the first tested payload fraction at which echo hiding becomes
audible for each representative cover category.

## Conditions

- Four representative PG-23 covers
- Requested payload fractions: 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, and 125%
- Fixed algorithm parameters
- Matched centered 12-second excerpts
- Randomized A/B identity and presentation order

## Required rating

```text
obvious
apparent_close_listening
undetectable_without_original
```

## Decision

The PG-13 perceptual threshold is the first tested fraction rated
`apparent_close_listening` or `obvious` for each cover. PG-24 is complete when
every selected file/setting has one of the three required ratings and complete
listener metadata.
